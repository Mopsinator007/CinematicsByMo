Behind The Lens

Mo shoots what

Cinematics by Mo is a one-camera, one-vision operation built around three subjects: the discipline of the gym, the texture of everyday life, and the pull of somewhere new.

Every project is shot, cut, and color graded by Mo directly — no hand-offs, no templates. What you see in the reel is the same eye that shows up to your shoot.

The Process

From call
to final cut

01

Discovery call

A quick call to talk through the goal — a gym launch reel, a lifestyle brand day, a travel edit — and lock the direction.

02

Shot list & scouting

Mo builds a shot list and checks the location or gym for light, space, and timing before shoot day.

03

Shoot day

On-site with a lean kit built for movement — handheld, gimbal, and long lens work depending on the subject.

04

Edit & color

A cinematic pass on pacing, sound design, and color grade — nothing goes out flat or unedited.

05

Delivery

Final files delivered in whatever cut you need — full film, social edits, and raw selects.
